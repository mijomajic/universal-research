# Evidence quality

Quality rules **change with domain**. Do not apply a medical evidence pyramid to a moodboard, or a Twitter engagement rank to a systems paper.

## Shared dimensions

For any source, note what you can:

- **Provenance** — who produced it, where you got it, can a reader retrieve it.
- **Primary vs secondary** — original measurement/code/filing vs someone else's retelling.
- **Recency** — does this question go stale.
- **Relevance** — right population, product, version, geography, time.
- **Independence** — not the same claim laundered through five blogs.
- **Incentives** — seller, advocate, journalist, scientist, anonymous user.
- **Anecdote vs systematic** — one vivid story vs a method that could have found otherwise.
- **Consensus vs contested** — agreement is not proof; disagreement is not a tie.
- **Missing evidence** — what you looked for and did not find.
- **Convergence** — independent methods pointing the same way.

Confidence language: `high` / `medium` / `low` / `unknown`, plus a one-line why. Prefer "low confidence because n=12 and no control" over fake precision.

## Science / biomedical

Weight roughly: systematic review/meta-analysis of RCTs > well-powered RCT > prospective controlled observational > small/uncontrolled > mechanism-only, animal, in vitro > anecdote. Preprints are evidence with extra uncertainty, not non-evidence.

Also weigh: controls, confounding, power, effect size vs practical significance, replication, publication bias, conflicts. Human data over animal for human claims. Do not upgrade a forum protocol into a trial.

## Academic (non-lab)

Weight seminal vs recent work, methods fit, primary texts vs textbooks, and whether a "school" is describing the same object. Citation count ≠ correctness. Avoid concluding from abstracts when the claim depends on methods or numbers in the full text.

## Market / customers

A repeating complaint across independent threads is a **signal**, not a prevalence. Reviews, interviews, support logs, and app-store text beat a single viral post. TAM figures without method are decoration. Pricing pages are primary for list price, not for willingness to pay.

## Technical

Prefer: current official docs, source, specs, reproducible benchmarks, issue trackers. Blog benchmarks are marketing until you see hardware, versions, and commands. Security claims need a defined threat model. Pin versions.

## Visual / content / social

Popularity ≠ preference of a target audience. Screenshots and pins need provenance and dedup. Engagement is platform-distorted. Treat social as qualitative unless the user asked for a platform-native snapshot ("what is X saying this week").

## Keyword / search

A SERP is a snapshot: engine, geo, language, personalization. Keyword tools are proxies. Informational vs commercial intent is a hypothesis until you open the results.

## Contradictions

Keep both sides when evidence conflicts. Explain likely reasons (population, date, method, incentive). Do not average them into a mushy consensus. If the honest answer is "insufficient evidence," say that.
